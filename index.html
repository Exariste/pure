Our Story

Built on one promise: nothing hidden in the bottle.

PURE OIL & SPICE began in Kushtia with a simple frustration — that "pure" had become a marketing word rather than a fact on the label. We set out to build a food brand where every claim could survive a lab test, and every ingredient could be traced back to a farm. Today that same discipline shapes our mustard oil, soybean oil, and stone-ground spices, made for households, distributors, and export partners who expect the same thing we do: honesty in every drop.
